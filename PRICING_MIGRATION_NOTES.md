## Margin policy

Do not market unlimited marginal-cost cloud resources. The catalog is intentionally structured to leave room for up to a 50% recurring affiliate residual while maintaining positive contribution margin. Actual affiliate payout automation and cloud overage billing are separate follow-up workstreams.
