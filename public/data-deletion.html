Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: April 30, 2026

ChatScream respects your privacy and gives you full control over your data. If you'd like to delete your account and all associated data, you can do so using any of the methods below.

Option 1: Delete From Your Account

  1. Log in to your ChatScream account at www.chatscream.live
  2. Go to Settings (click your profile icon)
  3. Scroll to Account and click Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

This will permanently delete your account, stream history, chat data, and all associated information.

Option 2: Email Request

Send an email to patriotnewsactivism@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Include:

  • The email address associated with your ChatScream account
  • Your ChatScream username (if applicable)

We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email once your data has been deleted.

Option 3: Facebook Login Users

If you signed up using Facebook Login, you can also remove ChatScream from your Facebook settings:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites
  2. Find ChatScream and click Remove
  3. Check the box to delete all data ChatScream received about you

This will trigger a deletion request on our end and we will remove all data linked to your Facebook account.

What Data Is Deleted?

When you request deletion, we permanently remove:

  • Your account profile and login credentials
  • Stream history and recordings
  • Chat messages and donation history
  • Connected platform tokens (YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, TikTok)
  • Any other personal data stored by ChatScream

Data Retention

After a deletion request is processed, your data is permanently removed from our active systems within 30 days. Backups containing your data may persist for up to 90 days before being overwritten.

Questions?

If you have any questions about data deletion, contact us at patriotnewsactivism@gmail.com.
